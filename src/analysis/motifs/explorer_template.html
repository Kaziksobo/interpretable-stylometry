How to read this table
Pattern
A fragment of a constituency parse. (VP (VBD) (SBAR)) is a verb phrase made of a past-tense verb and a subordinate clause, as in he said [that it rained]. A label with nothing inside stands for any structure below it. Depth is the number of levels in the fragment.
Rate
How often the pattern occurs per 1,000 tokens (words and punctuation) of that source's text. Tokens rather than sentences, because sentence length differs between sources.
log2 ratio
The AI rate divided by the human rate, on a log2 scale: +1 means twice as often, −1 half as often. The bar is clipped at ±3.
z
A ranking score for how well the data supports the difference: the log ratio divided by its approximate standard error. It treats occurrences as independent, so it overstates the evidence when a few documents account for most occurrences. Check the document counts, which show how widely a pattern is used. It is for ordering the table, not a significance test.
Both agree
Keeps patterns where GPT and Claude differ from human in the same direction, and ranks them by the weaker of the two scores.
Echo variants
A pattern's parent is the same pattern cut off one level higher. A variant whose log2 ratio is within the threshold of its parent's, and has the same sign, adds nothing new, so it is folded under the parent. Variants that differ from their parent stay in the list: they show where the parent's difference comes from. Open a row to see all of its variants.

The corpus is the Ghostbuster set. Counts are occurrences, so a pattern that occurs twice in a sentence counts twice. Only patterns used in at least documents of a domain are included.
